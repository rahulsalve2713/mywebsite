About

I'm a final-year Electronics & Computer Engineering student, and my work has a consistent shape: take a real-world signal — a camera feed, a voice recording, a sensor — and turn it into a system that does something useful with it.

That shape has taken me across the stack. A computer-vision cart that bills automatically. A RAG system that verifies whether a recording is authentic. A face-and-voice tracker that logs attendance in real time. Sometimes the answer needs a camera; sometimes it needs a language model. I pick whichever the problem actually calls for, then build the rest around it.

Right now I'm building a smart trolley — computer vision and IoT sensors on the cart, a Firebase backend, and a companion app for automated retail billing. I like problems where the boundary between hardware and software is the interesting part.

I'm looking for roles at product-based companies where that boundary matters — embedded systems, IoT, consumer electronics, or AI-powered products. What I bring: strong analytical thinking, systems-level problem solving, and a bias toward building over talking.
